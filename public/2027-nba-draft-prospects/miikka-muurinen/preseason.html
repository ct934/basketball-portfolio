Info

School: Arkansas | Class: FR | Position: PF | Height: 6'11" | Weight: 200 | Wingspan: 7'2"

Physical

He has a thin 6'11" frame with a long wingspan and good vertical explosiveness. While he needs to add strength, his length and athleticism provide an excellent foundation for long-term development.

Offense

His jump shot looks mechanically sound, but the production has been inconsistent on low volume. He has yet to show much self-creation ability and currently thrives more as a complementary offensive player. His biggest offensive asset right now is his high motor, consistently creating opportunities through effort, cuts, and hustle plays.

Defense

Despite his size and athleticism, he has not been the rim protector his tools suggest he could become. However, he brings strong energy on the defensive end, rebounds well, and consistently impacts possessions with his activity and effort.

Outlook

His development will largely depend on his offensive growth. If he becomes a reliable perimeter shooter, adds strength, and maintains the same motor on both ends of the floor, he has the upside to enter the top-five discussion as one of the most intriguing two-way forwards in the class.
