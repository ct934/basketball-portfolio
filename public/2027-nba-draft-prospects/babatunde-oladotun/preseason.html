Info

School: Maryland | Class: FR | Position: SF | Height: 6'10" | Weight: 195 | Wingspan: 7'1"

Physical

He moves exceptionally well for a 6'10" forward. Despite his thin frame, he is fluid, agile, and comfortable handling the ball in space. He lacks strength at this stage, but his mobility and body control are standout traits that give him intriguing long-term upside.

Offense

The biggest concern is his perimeter shooting. He was inefficient from three, and his mechanics still need refinement. While he looks comfortable taking jumpers, it remains to be seen how that translates against bigger, longer defenders.

Where he stands out is with the ball in his hands. He has impressive handle for his size and is a crafty finisher who can navigate through traffic and score creatively around the rim.

Defense

The flashes are encouraging, but the sample size is limited. He has shown the ability to rebound and block shots, though consistency will depend on his motor and physical development. With his size and mobility, he has the tools to defend multiple positions once he adds strength.

Outlook

He has one of the wider ranges in the class. His shooting development and physical growth will largely determine whether he becomes a lottery pick or slips into the 20s. If those areas improve, he projects as a versatile two-way forward who can fill a valuable 3-and-D role with additional offensive upside.
