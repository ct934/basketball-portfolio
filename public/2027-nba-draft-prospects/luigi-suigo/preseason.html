Physical

He has a rare physical profile at 7'4" and 289 pounds, yet moves exceptionally well for his size. He gets off the floor quickly, runs well in transition, and shows impressive mobility and lateral agility. His frame still has room to add strength, giving him even more long-term upside.

Offense

He has shown encouraging touch from three-point range with solid mechanics, even if the volume remains low. Around the basket, he is a surprisingly crafty finisher who uses his size and footwork well. The next step is developing a reliable go-to move in the paint—similar to how Zach Edey built his interior scoring package. Having a dependable option he can consistently rely on would elevate his offensive ceiling.

Defense

His combination of size, mobility, and instincts gives him the potential to be one of the best rim protectors in college basketball next season. He has already shown the ability to move with guards in international competition, and it will be interesting to see how that translates against high-level college guards in space. His physical tools allow him to impact shots without sacrificing mobility.

Outlook

If he develops a consistent interior scoring package while continuing to improve as a perimeter shooter, he has the potential to become one of the toughest matchups in college basketball. His rare blend of size, athleticism, and defensive upside makes him a strong candidate to remain firmly in the top-10 conversation.
