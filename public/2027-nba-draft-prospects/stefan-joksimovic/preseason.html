Info

Club: Saski Baskonia (Spain) | Position: PG | Height: 6'7" | Weight: 190 | Wingspan: 6'8"

Physical

He has an intriguing physical profile at 6'7" with excellent positional size for a lead guard. He is around an 8/10 athlete, showing good vertical pop, long strides, and fluid movement in the open floor. His frame is still thin, and adding strength will be one of the biggest factors in his long-term development.

Offense

He is an aggressive downhill guard who excels attacking out of pick-and-rolls and using pace changes to get into the paint. Despite his size, he handles the ball well and finishes with power when he has a runway. His shooting mechanics are promising, and he is comfortable pulling from NBA range, but his release is slow and starts from his chest, making it easier to contest. Improving his shooting consistency and adding strength to finish through contact will be critical as he faces more physical defenders.

Defense

His size and length give him solid defensive upside. He is active in passing lanes, can rotate over for weak-side blocks, and has the tools to defend multiple backcourt positions. While the physical tools are there, he has yet to consistently show the impact of an elite defender. Continued strength development should allow him to better handle physical matchups.

Outlook

He has a similar developmental profile to Egor Demin—a tall, skilled guard with shooting potential, positional size, and the ability to attack downhill. The upside is significant, but he is still more of a projection than a finished product. If the jumper and frame continue to develop, he has the tools to become a high-level NBA starter.
