Scouting + Analytics

Transfer Portal Scouting & Player Evaluation

Conducted comprehensive scouting and analytical evaluations of transfer portal prospects to identify players that matched coaching staff roster needs and desired positional archetypes. Combined statistical analysis with film study to assess each prospect’s production, efficiency, and overall playing style, ensuring on-court performance aligned with quantitative data.

Developed detailed scouting reports highlighting player strengths, weaknesses, fit within team schemes, and projected impact. Ranked prospects on evaluation boards based on overall fit, upside, and coaching priorities, then presented finalized reports and player boards to coaching staff to support recruiting and roster-building decisions.

Key Focus Areas

  • Statistical profile analysis and efficiency evaluation
  • Film breakdown to validate data against game performance
  • Archetype and scheme-fit assessment
  • Prospect ranking and board development
  • Communication of scouting insights to coaching staff
